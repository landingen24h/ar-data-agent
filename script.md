# The Lion and the Mouse

**Premise:** A big lion learns a tiny friend can be a great help.

Adapted from the Aesop fable *The Lion and the Mouse* (public domain). Audience: children 4 to 8. Total: 56 seconds in 8 scenes.

## Scene 1 (7 s, 0 to 7)

- **Shot:** Wide shot of bright savanna, slow pan left
- **We see:** A large lion lies curled on a warm rock, eyes closed, gentle breeze rustles grass
- **Narration:** A calm morning
- **Dialogue:** (none)
- **Sound:** soft wind, distant birds

## Scene 2 (7 s, 7 to 14)

- **Shot:** Medium shot, camera follows mouse as it darts near lion, then pans to lion paw
- **We see:** A tiny brown mouse scurries across grass, the lion lifts a paw and gently catches it
- **Narration:** The lion catches the mouse
- **Dialogue:** Mouse: Please let me go
- **Sound:** quick footsteps, light rustle

## Scene 3 (7 s, 14 to 21)

- **Shot:** Close-up on lion and mouse, slight push in
- **We see:** The mouse trembles, the lion chuckles softly and opens his paw
- **Narration:** The lion laughs and releases the mouse
- **Dialogue:** Lion: I will let you go
- **Sound:** soft chuckle, gentle sigh

## Scene 4 (7 s, 21 to 28)

- **Shot:** Wide shot, static camera as lion walks near bushes, rope net lies hidden
- **We see:** The lion steps onto a thin rope net, the ropes tighten around his paws
- **Narration:** Later the lion is caught
- **Dialogue:** (none)
- **Sound:** soft snap of rope

## Scene 5 (7 s, 28 to 35)

- **Shot:** Medium shot, camera tilts up to lion's face, then pans to grass
- **We see:** The lion growls low, a small mouse nearby lifts ears and looks toward the sound
- **Narration:** The mouse hears the lion
- **Dialogue:** Mouse: I will help!
- **Sound:** low growl, tiny footsteps

## Scene 6 (7 s, 35 to 42)

- **Shot:** Close-up on mouse teeth, slow zoom out to show rope
- **We see:** The mouse nibbles the rope, fibers break and loosen
- **Narration:** (none)
- **Dialogue:** (none)
- **Sound:** soft nibble, tearing thread

## Scene 7 (7 s, 42 to 49)

- **Shot:** Wide shot, camera pulls back as rope falls, lion stands free
- **We see:** The rope snaps, the lion steps out, the mouse hops nearby, both smile
- **Narration:** Friends rescue each other
- **Dialogue:** (none)
- **Sound:** light rustle, happy chime

## Scene 8 (7 s, 49 to 56)

- **Shot:** Medium shot, gentle pan right as lion nudges mouse
- **We see:** The lion gently nudges the mouse, they walk together across the grass
- **Narration:** (none)
- **Dialogue:** (none)
- **Sound:** soft breeze, birds

## Characters

### Lion

- Look: Large tawny lion, golden mane, soft brown eyes, calm expression
- Voice: Deep gentle voice

### Mouse

- Look: Small brown mouse, bright eyes, twitching whiskers, tiny paws
- Voice: High sweet voice

Moral shown, not preached: Kindness comes back.
